How to PlayTHE RULES

🎲
Roll the cube across the grid with the arrows (or swipe / arrow keys). It tips one square at a time, and it remembers which face is down.
🩶
Every square the cube lands on gets painted. Your goal: paint every floor square on the grid.
✦
A glowing moon-pad ✦ only locks when the cube's gold face is the one rolling down onto it. The other faces are silver.
◎
Finish standing on the goal ◎ with the whole grid lit. That completes the level.
❄
Ice tiles are slick — the cube keeps rolling once it touches one, until it hits a wall, an edge, or plain floor again.
↺
Stuck? Reset the level anytime, or rotate the view to see the board from another angle. Beat the par for a clean solve.
